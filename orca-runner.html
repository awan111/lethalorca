How To Play

  • 1
    The orca swims forward automatically — you control lane changes and breach-jumps.
  • 2
    On laptop: Arrow keys / A,D to change lanes, Space or Up to breach (jump). On mobile: swipe left/right for lanes, tap anywhere to breach.
  • 3
    Steer into floating LORCA coins worth 2,000, 5,000 or 10,000 — gold coins are worth the most.
  • 4
    Time your breach over reef rocks and debris. Glowing mines float too high to clear — dodge those by changing lanes instead.
  • 5
    Distance milestones raise your Level, speed and reward tier automatically. Each run lasts 1 minute — the clock is always ticking at the top of the screen.
  • 6
    After a run, open Rewards and submit your wallet address (with an optional screenshot) to claim your LORCA.
